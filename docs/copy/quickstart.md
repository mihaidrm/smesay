# Quickstart

Shown once after the first sign-in (once per person per workspace), and always under Help in
the sidebar footer. Four steps on one page, each a card. Nothing
below describes a feature the product does not have (R1 scope, stories/backlog.md).

## Page copy

Title: Your first validation in four steps

Intro: Upload the list you were about to email round. In about ten minutes it is a link your
experts can answer on their phones. Try it on the sample project first if you want to see the
end before the start.

Step 1, Import the list
Upload an xlsx or csv, or paste the list. Tell us which column is the requirement and which is
the priority. We read the rest. The file stays as it is; you can re-import a newer version
later and nothing you have done is lost.

Step 2, Shape it
The AI groups the items into areas and writes a readable version of each one. Accept or reject
each suggestion. The original wording is always kept and shown to you. Nothing changes until
you press Accept. Add a few words about the project at the top of Import so the AI keeps your
names and terms as written.

Step 3, Build the instrument
Pick how people rate (Must, Should, Could, Not needed; 1 to 5 fit; or keep, change, drop),
whether they see your proposed value, and which fields they fill in (name and role by
default). The preview on the right shows what they will see, on a desktop or a phone.

Step 4, Share one link
Publish a public link, or send personal invites that resume on any device. Set when it closes.
Respondents need no account. You can close or withdraw the link at any time.

Then: read the results
Answers arrive live. For every item you see who agreed, who pushed back and why, who asked a
question, and what people said is missing. The AI writes a short to-do list from the answers
and names the answers behind each line. Export the answers as CSV, the project as JSON, or a
PDF summary.

Button: Start a project
Secondary link: Open the sample project
