# Outreach and launch drafts

Every text Mihai sends or posts, in one place, so the words are checked once (WRITING.md,
`npm run scan:copy`). Square brackets are filled in per person. Nothing here says "AI" as a
selling point on its own (decision 0046); the AI is named for what it does.

## The one-liners

Tagline (under 60 characters): Send your requirements as a link. See who agrees.

Short description (under 260 characters): SMEsay turns a requirements list into a link. The
people who know the work answer item by item, with reasons. You see where they agree, where
they do not, and get a to-do list drafted from their answers. No account for them, phone
first.

Long description (for directories that take 500 or more characters): You wrote down what a new
tool or process must do. Getting the people who know the work to agree is a spreadsheet by
email, a form you built yourself, or a workshop nobody can schedule. SMEsay imports the list
you already have, rewrites each item in plain words (you choose the wording), and sends it
as one link. Your experts open it on a phone, no account, and answer item by item: agree, a
different priority, not needed, or a question, and why. You watch the answers arrive, see
who disagrees and why, and get a to-do list drafted from the answers with the answers behind
each line. Export the CSV or the PDF. Free while we build it with the first users.
