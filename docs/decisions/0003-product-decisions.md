# 0003 Product decisions from Mihai, 2026-09-30

1. Showing the proposed value to respondents is an option per instrument. The PM chooses,
   when building the instrument, whether respondents see the proposed priority (or other proposed
   value) for each item or rate blind. Both modes feed the same dashboard.

2. The respondent journey ends on a wrap-up page where the respondent reviews what they said and
   gives their sign-off: a summary of their answers, the items they pushed back on or flagged,
   the closing questions, and an explicit confirmation step that marks the response as submitted.
   The wording of the sign-off is configurable by the PM.

3. Respondent side: no accounts, autosave, resume, finish in one sitting on a phone. Visual polish
   is spent on the PM side; the respondent side is optimised for speed and clarity.

4. Enterprise path: single-tenant hosted first, self-hosted package later; both designed for from
   day one (see 0001).
