## Example used in every prototype: a company replacing its expense tool

Mihai asked for an example everyone understands at once. Expenses are something every office
worker has submitted and every manager has approved. The invented company is Marlow Group, 400
staff. Six requirements in three areas: Submitting (receipt photo, split receipts), Approving
(approve from email, policy flags), Paying (paid with salary, cash advances). Seven invented
respondents: two in Sales, Finance, an engineering manager, HR, an office manager, and one who
has not started. The numbers are unchanged from the earlier example: 30 answers, 20 agree, 8
pushed back, 2 unclear, 1 missing item. The conflict shown is Sales against everyone else.

The earlier example (a veterinary group choosing a clinic system) is replaced on the user
journey, the PM app, the respondent flow and landing page C. Landing pages A and B keep the old
example; they are superseded.
