Decision (Claude under decision 0044, recorded here and in docs/review-list.md):
- The strategy is docs/marketing/launch-strategy.md: founder-led outreach to operations, IT
  and project people at companies of 10 to 250 and to implementation consultants; one
  bottom-of-funnel page a week; the respondent link as the loop; directories in Launch week;
  no ad spend before the first paying customer; Product Hunt in R2 as the plan already says.

  never spends, and never posts under Mihai's name without his go on that text (decision
  0039 and the session rules).
- Mihai owns the accounts (LinkedIn company page, Product Makers, the directories), every
  post under his name, every call, the go on each outreach batch, and pressing Launch.
- The first two real users come from Mihai's network first, Product Makers second, LinkedIn
  third, consultants fourth (docs/marketing/pilot-users.md), with the offer of a year of Pro
  and a 30-minute call.

Why: the sources agree that the first customers of a bootstrapped B2B product come from the
founder's own messages, not from ads or broad content; the product already has the loop
(every respondent sees "Powered by SMEsay") and the measurement (the admin funnel, E13-2);
what is missing is the routine, which Claude can run every day.
