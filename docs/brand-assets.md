## Recommendation

Buy nothing until the name is decided. A logo is tied to the name, and everything else on the
list has a free option that is good enough for R1.

After the name is decided, buy one thing: a logo with copyright transferred in writing. Expected
spend: USD 249 to 432.

| Option | Price | What you get | Catch |

| Brandmark generator (https://brandmark.io/pricing/) | USD 35, 95 or 195, one-off | Vector files from a generator; the USD 95 tier adds source files | Built from a shared icon library, so another company can end up with a similar mark. Weak for a trademark. |

Claude's pick: 99designs Bronze if you want a mark made for the name; the USD 400 Dribbble
premade if you see a mark you like. Skip the generators: the placeholder already does their job.

## Imagery

No purchase needed for R1 beyond the mascot of docs/assets.md (decision 0041). The design uses product screens on cards (design note 02; v2 in note 33).

- Free: Unsplash photos (https://unsplash.com/license) allow commercial use without credit.
  Architecture, paper and landscape photos fit the style. Other sites can use the same photo.
- Cheap, only if the landing page feels flat after first users: neutral grain and gradient packs
  on Creative Market, USD 9 to 22 each, for example "Neutral Gradients Collection"
  (https://creativemarket.com/GraphicCo/7161282-Neutral-Gradients-Collection) and "Ethereal
  Neutrals Abstract Gradients"
  (https://creativemarket.com/SunnyAfternoons/6262387-Ethereal-Neutrals-Abstract-Gradients).
  Check the licence first: the Creative Market commercial licence text does not list marketing
  websites and excludes web apps (https://creativemarket.com/licenses/general). Ask their
  support in writing before buying.
- Subscription to avoid unless needed: Envato Elements, USD 39 for one month. Every download
  must be registered to a project before cancelling or the licence does not cover it.

## Free and good enough

- Typeface: Geist, SIL Open Font License (https://github.com/vercel/geist-font).
- Icons: Lucide, ISC licence (https://lucide.dev/license).
- Browser and phone frames for screenshots: Claude builds them in CSS. No purchase, no licence.

## How buying works

placeholder, updates the favicon and the email header, and records the licence in this file.
