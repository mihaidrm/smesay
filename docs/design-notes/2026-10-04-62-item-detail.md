# Design note 62: the item detail panel, 2026-10-04

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-04 for stories/E8-5, under decision 0044.
The PM app board's item detail panel is the layout; this note records what was decided
while building it.

## What was decided

- The frame is a 560 px region fixed to the right of the viewport over Results, the
  surface colour with a hairline on its left and the card shadow (DetailShell in
  src/app/app/(shell)/projects/[projectId]/results/detail-shell.tsx). It is a dialog that
  leaves the page usable (role dialog, aria-modal false: developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/
  Accessibility/ARIA/Reference/Roles/dialog_role), not the modal sheet the story's
  technical notes named, so the filter bar and the tab behind stay in reach and the counts
  follow a filter change. Not in the design system before.
- The item is in the URL (item=[id]) beside the filter and the tab, so the panel is a link
  within the workspace; Close is a link to the same URL without the item, and Escape goes
  there too. The heading takes the focus when the panel opens.
- The pills are the design system's status pills for the four kinds (Different priority in
  the pushed back tint, decision 0044 item 6) and the neutral pill for Rated, In progress,
  Not started, Not answered and Not submitted.
- Rows: the answers first, then the people without one, each group by name. A value shows
  only where it differs from the proposal; a rate-blind list shows every value.
- The counts line is mono in muted ink, like the coverage figures of the Agreement table.
