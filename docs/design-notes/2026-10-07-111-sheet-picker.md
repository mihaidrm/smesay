# 111 The Sheets step on Import, 2026-10-07

Mihai, 2026-10-07: "what happens if the excel has multiple tabs and the requirements are in
just 1 or 2 of those tabs? We should add a step - if we detect file has more tabs, we ask
users which tabs we import and map - and he selects all tabs that apply". Story E3-7.

Before this note, a workbook with several sheets opened on the first sheet with rows and a
"Sheet / Show sheet" dropdown let the PM swap to another; one sheet was imported. The
dropdown is gone. In its place, when the workbook has more than one sheet with at least one
non-empty row, the Preview card opens with a Sheets step: each such sheet as a checkbox with
its count of non-empty rows in brackets ("Requirements (7 rows)"; the header row counts, since it is found after the pick), the first ticked, and "Use these sheets".
Until it is pressed, the summary line reads "Tick the sheets that hold the list, then press Use
these sheets." and the mapping and check cards are not rendered; an empty sheet is not listed.
A csv, a pasted list and a workbook with one sheet with rows never see the step.

With one sheet ticked the card is the E3-2 card. With several, each ticked sheet has a block
under a hairline: "Sheet [NAME]: [N] rows, header on row [N]." (or "no header row" with the
E3-2 message), its own Header row picker, and its first ten rows under its own column
headers; the summary reads "We read [N] rows from [FILE] across [N] sheets." The step stays
above the blocks, with a secondary button, so the PM can change the ticks.

The mapping card lists the columns of every ticked sheet once, by header, first seen first,
with the letter of the sheet it was first seen in; one mapping covers every ticked sheet. A
ticked sheet whose header lacks the item text column is named in the card's error line and the
Import button stays disabled: "Sheet [NAME] has no column [HEADER]. Untick it, or map the item
text to a column every ticked sheet has." Under the rows, when no column is mapped as Area and
several sheets are ticked, a switch "Use the sheet names as areas" (on by default; the design
system's Switch, submitted like a select change) with the line "Each item's area is the name
of the sheet it came from." or, off, "No area column is mapped; the items come in without an
area." Mapping an Area column hides the switch and the column wins.

The check card repeats its three counts once per sheet under "Sheet [NAME]", the rows named
as in that sheet; a duplicate of a row on another sheet reads "Row 2, same as row 2 of sheet
Requirements". The log's Checks cell ends in ", [N] sheets"; Source stays "xlsx".

Rejected: a mapping per sheet (one list, one mapping; a sheet that differs is unticked and
imported as the next version), a merged "Sheet" column on the items (the area is where tabs
already carry their meaning, and the item keeps its sheet's row number in the check only), and
keeping the dropdown next to the step (two controls for one choice).

Looked at: no outside product; the step follows the card's own pickers (E3-2) and the share
form's checkboxes (E6-2), with the respondent checkbox styling of src/components/respondent.
