# Design note 45: the Closing card and the Wrap up preview, 2026-10-03

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-03 for stories/E5-5, under decision 0044.
Files: src/lib/closing.ts (the rule and the words), src/lib/instruments.ts (saveClosing),
the Build page's closing-form.tsx and preview-screen.tsx, src/components/respondent/wrap-up.tsx,
build/preview-panel.tsx.

## What was decided

- ClosingSpec gains the optional closingQuestion (INTERFACES.md first, then
  src/db/types.ts); no migration, the column is jsonb and the old rows' "" for signOffText
  reads as the default sentence (signOffFor). The sign-off is stored as written once the PM
  saves, the default included, so a later change of the default never rewords a published
  instrument.
- Confidence is always asked (decision 0003): the card shows the row with a pill "Always
  on" instead of a switch that cannot move, and the server refuses a spec with it off.
- The PM's words are theirs: the server trims, checks the lengths and refuses an em dash,
  the one rule the copy scan would fail on; no banned-word check on their text.
- Once published the closing question locks like the method, since its answers are stored
  per response (E7-5); the missing-item switch and the sign-off text still change.
  Recorded in docs/review-list.md.
- The Wrap up follows the respondent board (note 12): the tally tiles (five with the
  proposed value shown, three rate-blind), the amber gaps box with "Go to [CHAPTER]", the
  missing-item form (text, area, value) when on, the closing question when set, confidence
  as five 48 px pills with Guessing and Certain under the ends, the sign-off as one label
  with a checkbox, and Submit disabled with "Still needed: ..." or "Everything is in.
  Submit when you are ready." In the preview nothing is answered: the tally is zero and
  every visible item is still to finish. E7-5 renders the same component with the answers.
- The preview's screen switch gains Wrap up, and the Closing card opens it when any of its
  controls takes focus or is clicked (a React context over the Build page's two columns,
  keyed on the instrument; React's onFocus bubbles, and the click covers a browser that
  does not focus a button on click). Nothing switches back on blur.
- Amended after the audit of 2026-10-03: a stale tab posting another question after
  publishing gets the locked message instead of "Saved."; the confidence pills are one
  radio group with a roving tabindex, arrow keys and Guessing and Certain described on 1
  and 5; a form part that is not text reads as empty; the preview says "Submit is off in
  the preview." once everything is picked, and "No items to review." when the respondent
  can see no item. The Wrap up rings the four parts the card
  changes as one group, with its own caption under the switch (decision 0021); four
  rings 16 px apart touched each other.
