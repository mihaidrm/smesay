# Design note 46: the public link, Share and the respondent link pages, 2026-10-03

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-03 for stories/E6-1, under decision 0044.
Files: src/lib/sharing.ts and sharing-copy.ts (the rule and the words), src/lib/passcode.ts
(the hash), src/lib/link-access.ts (what a link shows), src/db/queries/invites.ts (publish
under the lock), src/db/queries/links.ts (the read by token), src/db/queries/instruments.ts
(updateLocked), the Share page (share/page.tsx, share-form.tsx, copy-link.tsx), the
respondent route (src/app/r/[token]/) and src/components/respondent/link-page.tsx.

## What was decided

- One invite row of kind public per instrument, created by Publish under the instrument
  row's lock, the lock the four locking saves now take as well (saveScoring narrows to the
  layout, saveClosing keeps the question, savePerspectives and tagItem refuse). The test
  holds the lock on a second connection, starts the three saves, publishes and checks each
  outcome, which closes the race recorded after E5-4 and E5-5.
- The token is 32 hex characters from crypto.randomBytes(16); the link is
  [BASE URL]/r/[token], the base URL being BETTER_AUTH_URL (the app's own address).
- The respondent side reads by token only: links.byToken joins the invite to its
  instrument, project and workspace brand and returns the workspace id as a WorkspaceId,
  so the page can call the scoped helpers for the items. No session, no listing. An
  archived project's link reads as unknown.
- Dates: the Share form uses datetime-local inputs in the browser's zone, names the zone
  under them and posts ISO instants; the workspace has no zone of its own. The respondent
  pages write the instants in UTC ("6 Oct 2026, 09:00 UTC"). A close date is required to
  publish and must be in the future; afterwards it can be moved into the past, which
  closes the link at once (the story's own Playwright step).
- The passcode is a salted scrypt hash from Node core (no dependency; the story named
  argon2 or bcrypt). The passcode page sets a cookie scoped to /r/[token] holding an HMAC
  of the token and the stored hash under the app's secret: it proves the passcode was typed
  on this device, says nothing about it, and dies when the PM changes or removes the
  passcode. The passcode can be set, changed or removed after publishing. Attempt limits
  are E11-1's.
- Every link state is a page with the words of docs/copy/errors.md: unknown, not yet
  open, closed, inactive (revoked, for E6-4), the passcode step, and the About you page
  itself (the component the Build preview draws; Start and the device token are E7-1's).
  The closed page shows no contact sentence on a public link; the sender of a personal
  invite is E6-2's.
- The stepper's Share step: current once the link exists, Build done then. The projects
  list reads Open from the link's dates as before (src/lib/project-status.ts). The Import
  page shows the banner owed from E3-6 once the newest instrument has a link.

## Amended after the audit of 2026-10-03

- One link in force per project: publishing a newer instrument closes the older link at
  that instant; the Share page shows the link in force and a second card for a newer draft;
  the stepper and the Import banner read the link in force; Share's own() accepts the
  instrument holding it. instrument.published_at is recorded (migration 0015).
- The project list says Scheduled for a link that opens later; Share's note says when.
- The passcode hash is async scrypt with N = 2^17, r = 8, p = 1 and stores its parameters;
  the proof key is derived from the app's secret for this one use; the cookie is compared
  by bytes; the passcode step runs only on an open link; wrong attempts are limited in the
  process (5 per link and address in 15 minutes) until E11-1.
- The Share form fills its dates and the zone name only in the browser (the server render
  shows them empty), so the two renders agree; the form remounts when the passcode changes,
  so a typed passcode is not hashed twice.
- The About you header says "Closes [DATE] UTC"; an archived project's link reads as
  closed on the day it was archived; publishing an archived project is refused; the
  closed and not-open pages show the names before any passcode (docs/review-list.md).
- saveClosing compares the posted question with the row as locked.
