# Design note 12: respondent prototype rebuilt around chapters, 2026-10-01

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-01 after Mihai's direction (decision 0016) and
the reviewer's audit of the first version (note 11). Files: docs/design-notes/prototype-01/
Respondent.dc.html (390 by 844), RespondentDesktop.dc.html (1440 by 900), both generated by
respondent-generator.py (Python 3.12 or later), checked by respondent-sim.js (node, 42 checks).

## What the boards show

- Header with the PM's name and a saved, not saved, submitted, closed or inactive note.
- Chapter row: About you, Submitting, Approving, Paying, Wrap up, each with its count. Free
  navigation. A 4 px progress bar under it in the PM's accent.
- About you: intro, name and role (not asked on a personal link), a welcome-back box when the
  device or the personal link has answers, Powered by SMEsay.
- Chapter screen: title, one-line intro, compact cards. A card is a fieldset whose legend is the
  reference and the item text; the proposed value; one row of four pills (Agree, Change,
  Disagree, Unclear); a grey panel that opens for the priority choice and the reason, or the
  question; a note line that says exactly what is missing, or Saved. Rate-blind cards show the
  four priorities on one row and Disagree, Unclear on the next.
- Footer: Back, Continue to the next chapter (never blocked), and how many are left here.
- Wrap up: summary line, an amber box naming the gaps with a button to the chapter, the review
  list (status pill, Change, the reason), the optional missing item, the optional closing
  question marked as PM-configurable, confidence 1 to 5, the sign-off as one 48 px label,
  Submit with a line listing what is still needed, a Submitting state.
- Done, Link closed, Link inactive. The closed page shows the respondent's own state only when
  the link was personal.
- Prototype strip: layout (chapters, one item per screen), proposed value (shown, hidden), link
  (public first visit, public same device again, personal, closed, revoked), connection.

## Reviewer findings from note 11 and what happened to each

Fixed in this version: 4 (typed text kept when switching answer), 5 (personal seed follows
blind mode), 6 and 7 (blind toggle resets the flow; Submit needs every item), 8 (open link
starts fresh), 9 (resume lands on the first unanswered chapter, count computed), 10 (Change my
answers clears the confirmation), 11 and 12 (Wrap up is one chapter away from everything; the
missing item is edited in place), 13 (closing question and sign-off wording marked as
PM-configurable), 14 and 15 (48 px targets, the sign-off row is one label), 16 (not answered is
a dashed white pill, token added to the design system), 18 to 21 (tokens: 24/30 titles, 17/26
body, 12 px pills, 4 px progress, hairline #E6E4DF, radii 12 and 999, 4 px grid), 22 (hover
and a Submitting state), 23 (Powered by), 24 (offline state), 25 (returning public link on the
same device), 27 and 28 (notes say what is missing and update), 29 and 30 (label headings,
Continue), 31 and 32 (copy), 33 (closed page shows state only for personal links), 35 and 36
(fieldset and legend per item, aria-live notes, progressbar role, Change buttons named),
37 (dead code), 3 (generator header and output path), 1 in part (the simulation is committed
and re-runnable; the template still renders only in the canvas runtime).

Not done, by design or waiting on Mihai:
- 17: the focus ring stays on :focus-visible (keyboard). The design system now says so; a
  ring on every mouse click is noise.
- 26: the closed page no longer states a policy on partial responses. Whether unsubmitted
  answers reach the PM, and how they are marked, is a decision for Mihai (listed in context).
- 33: whether a closed public link may show any per-device state at all is the same decision.
- 34: self-hosting Geist is a build task for E1, recorded in docs/design-system.md.
- 2: no story file exists for the prototypes; stories come with Phase 2 (decision 0013).
