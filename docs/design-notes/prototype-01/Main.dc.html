SMEsay
User journey, draft 2

From a requirement list to agreed actions

Two people, one link. The PM prepares and publishes at step 4. Respondents answer on a phone. Results arrive at step 5 while the link is still open.
Product manager
Desktop, signed in
Open the PM prototype
01
Import the list
Upload an xlsx or csv. Map the columns once.
02
Shape it
AI groups items into areas and suggests a readable version. You accept or reject each one.
03
Build the instrument
Pick the scoring method, the respondent fields and whether the proposed value is shown.
04
Share one link
Publish a public link or send personal invites. Close or revoke it at any time.
05
Read the results
Agreement per item, changed and disagreed items with their reasons, open questions and gaps.
06
Act on it
Work through AI-written actions. Each one cites the responses behind it.
Respondent
Phone, no account
Open the respondent prototype
A
Open the link
Read the short intro from the PM. Nothing to install or sign up for.
B
Say who you are
Name and role, or whichever fields the PM asked for.
C
Answer a chapter at a time
Each screen is one AI-grouped chapter with compact cards. Agree, change the priority, disagree or ask, with a reason every time.
D
Move freely
The chapter row at the top shows what is done. Come back to any chapter, in any order.
E
Wrap up and sign off
Add what is missing, check every answer, rate your confidence, confirm.
F
Submitted
Answers stay editable until the close date. Progress is saved on every answer.
