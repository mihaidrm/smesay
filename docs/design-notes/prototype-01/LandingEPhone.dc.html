How it works

Three steps. Each one ends with something you can show.
01Upload and tidy
Tell it which column is the requirement and which the priority. It groups the items into areas and rewrites each one in plain words. The original is always kept and nothing changes until you press Accept.
You getA list people can read
02Send one link
A public link or personal invites, with open and close dates and a passcode if you want one. No account for respondents. Answers save as they go.
You getAnswers, with reasons
03Read and decide
Agreement per item and area, the push-backs with their reasons, the questions, the gaps. A to-do list written from the answers, each item citing them.
You getA to-do list and a record
