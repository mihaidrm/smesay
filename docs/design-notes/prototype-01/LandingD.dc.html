Step 2

Send one link

Your experts open the link and go through the list: agree, pick a different priority and say why, or say what is unclear. A reason is required, so you never get a bare no. At the end they confirm their answers, and you have a record. No account, works on a phone, and they can stop and come back.
Step 3

See who agrees, who does not, and why

Every item shows how many agreed, pushed back or found it unclear. Every push-back shows its reason and who gave it. AI reads all of it and writes a short to-do list, naming the answers each item came from. Export it to a spreadsheet or a PDF.

Why not a survey form?

Eighty items are readable
A form with eighty questions gets abandoned. Here each item is one screen, with the area and the progress always visible, and people can stop and come back.
You get reasons, not votes
A form counts answers. Here nobody can disagree without saying why, and nobody can mark an item unclear without asking the question.
You know what to do next
A form gives you a spreadsheet of results. Here you get a to-do list written from the answers, with the answers it came from, and a record of who signed off.
