# Design note 96: dropdowns, 2026-10-05

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-05, under decisions 0044 and 0056, after
Mihai's screenshot of the About you dropdown (decision 0056 quotes it).

## What was decided

- Every native dropdown in the app has the same closed box: the browser's arrow is turned off
  (appearance: none, developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/appearance) and Lucide's chevron-down,
  the icon set of the rest of the app, is drawn 16 px wide and 16 px from the right edge, in
  ink-muted (#5E5A72 light, #A8A4BE dark). The text keeps 44 px clear of the right edge, so a
  long value never runs under the chevron.
- The open list, where the browser supports a customizable select (appearance: base-select,
  developer.mozilla.org/docs/Learn_web_development/Extensions/Forms/Customizable_select), is a
  menu as docs/design-system.md draws menus: the surface colour, a hairline-strong border, the
  12 px radius and the card shadow, 6 px below the box, 4 px inside. Options are 8 by 12 px
  with an 8 px radius (the menu's 12 less its 4 px inset, so the curves stay parallel), violet-soft on hover and focus, the chosen one in 600. The browser's
  check mark is hidden, since the box above already names the choice.
- Where the browser has no customizable select (Safari and Firefox on 2026-10-05, per the MDN
  page above), the closed box is the same and the open list is the system's. Phones open their
  own picker, which fits the hand better than a menu.

## Where

One unlayered block at the end of src/app/globals.css, so it wins over the utilities' padding
(developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS/@layer). It covers the 15 native single selects in 12
files: About you and Wrap up (respondent), the import pickers and mapping, the fields form on
Build, the Shape board's Move to, the Results filter bar, agreement controls and gap field,
Settings members, and the two admin pages. The workspace switcher uses the base-ui Select
(src/components/ui/select.tsx), whose list is already a rounded card. The three boards that
draw a select (PmApp, Respondent, RespondentDesktop) draw the same chevron.

## Checked

Screenshots at 1440: the About you dropdown open in light and dark, and the compact (32 px)
Results selects closed and open. The Playwright specs that use these selects pass with
selectOption: respondent-start, admin-workspaces, import, build, results.
