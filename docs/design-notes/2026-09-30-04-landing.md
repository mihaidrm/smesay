## What the landing page takes from it

The section order and rhythm: image band, centred hero, logo strip, heading with three numerals,
three-column row, quote card, one full-bleed colour band, three product sections built the same
way, trust band, a four-card row, a dark footer. The type scale and spacing above, set in Geist.
Black pill buttons. One colour moment (the teal band).

## What is ours

- Typeface: Geist, not GT America.
- Imagery: no painting. The hero band and the footer use flat overlapping hill shapes built in
  CSS in greige, sage and teal-grey. The hero shows three product fragments on top of them.
  This follows decision 0004 (nothing hand-drawn).
- The colour band is the brand teal with four faint circles, not a sky.
- All copy. The row of four cards is pricing, not news.
- The numerals are facts of the design (1 link, 0 accounts, 100 percent of actions cite a
  response), not measured results. No outcome is claimed.

## Placeholders, marked in square brackets on the page

until a real organisation has run a validation. Prices come from the business plan.
