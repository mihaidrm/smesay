# Design note 03: user journey and first prototype, 2026-09-30

Superseded in part: item 1 (three answers) by decision 0014, items 2 and 3 (one item per screen,
the missing-item screen) by decision 0016 and note 12.

Made in a Claude Code session with Mihai. Nothing external was looked at in this session. The
inputs were docs/business-plan.pdf (epics E3 to E9), decision 0003, and design notes 01 and 02.

## What was made

A design canvas with three boards:
https://claude.ai/artifact/Y7ZpX3JPahVt8shJDzQx5d (private to Mihai).

1. User journey. Two lanes of six steps: the PM (import, shape, build, share, read results, act)
   and the respondent (open link, say who you are, rate items, add what is missing, review and
   sign off, submitted).
2. PM app, 1440 px wide, click-through. Five steps: Import, Shape, Build, Share, Results.
3. Respondent on a phone, 390 px wide, click-through from landing to submitted.

The source files are in docs/design-notes/prototype-01/. They use the canvas file format, not
the app stack. None of this is application code. Claude did not open the boards in a browser
after writing them; Mihai's first click-through is the first check.

## Mocked data

An invented veterinary group ("Harbour Vets", five clinics) choosing a clinic system. Six
requirements in three areas, seven invented respondents, five submitted. No client material.
The dashboard numbers are computed from the mocked answers: 30 answers, 20 agree (67 percent),
8 pushed back, 2 unclear, 1 missing item.

## Decisions made in the prototype, for Mihai to confirm

1. Answer model when the proposed value is shown: three choices per item. "Agree", "Should be
   different" (pick another MoSCoW value, reason mandatory), "Unclear" (question mandatory).
   When the proposed value is hidden, respondents pick the value directly.
2. One item per screen on the phone, with the area name and "2 of 6" progress at the top.
3. The missing-item form is its own screen after the last item, and can be skipped.
4. The summary screen lists every answer with a "Change" link, then the confidence scale
   (1 to 5, mandatory), then a sign-off checkbox, then "Submit answers". This follows decision
   0003 item 2.
5. Shaping: each AI reader version is shown above the original text with "Accept" and "Reject".
   Nothing changes without an accept. Ambiguity flags appear as a banner that can be dismissed.
6. The builder shows a live preview of one item that changes with the scoring method and the
   "show the proposed value" switch. Superseded 2026-10-01: a preview panel on every builder
   step (decision 0021, note 13).
7. Results: five headline numbers, then tabs for Agreement, Pushed back, Questions and gaps,
   Responses, Actions. The conflict view sits under the agreement table and compares one
   respondent field (role) across two groups.
8. Actions show a title, the reason, the responses cited, and "Mark done" or "Dismiss".

## Tokens used

From note 02: white body, light grey (#F6F6F4) sections, greige (#ECEAE5) cards, hairline
#E3E1DC, ink #16181C, black pill primary buttons, teal (#0E6B63) for links, focus rings and the
mark. Geist Sans and Geist Mono. PM side 14 px text and 6 px radius; respondent side 17 px text,
12 px radius, 48 px or taller tap targets.

Added in this note: status tints with dark text for labels, because white text on the data
colours fails contrast at small sizes. Agree #E6F4EC with #22643F; pushed back #FBF1DC with
#7A5210; unclear #EEE8FA with #4C2F94; missing #E3EEF9 with #1F4F7A; neutral #F0F0EE with #454A52.
The solid data colours from note 01 stay for bars and dots.

## Not in the prototype

Sign-in, workspace settings, personal-link resume, closed and revoked respondent pages, the
other two layouts (one area per screen, single long page), exports, dark mode, the landing page.

## Rejected

Figma: Claude has no signed-in Figma connection in this setup, and a canvas Mihai can click on
a phone does the same job. A separate artboard per screen: the flows need shared state, so each
flow is one board.
