# Design note 49: the kill switch, 2026-10-04

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-04 for stories/E6-4, under decision 0044.
Files: src/db/queries/invites.ts (revokePublic, revokePersonal, renewPersonal, publish and
updatePublic after a revoke), src/lib/sharing.ts (revokeLink), src/lib/invitees.ts
(revokeInvitee, renewInvitee), src/lib/link-access.ts (linkStatus), src/app/r/[token]/
state/route.ts and link-watch.tsx, the Share page (share/revoke-link.tsx,
invite-row-actions.tsx, invites-card.tsx, share-form.tsx).

## What was decided

- "Revoke link" is one press with the hint in front of it, no confirmation: a kill switch
  has to be fast, and the harm of a wrong press is a new link (Publish again), not lost
  answers. The card reads Revoked at once, the link row and Copy link go, and the form
  turns into Publish again with empty dates and no passcode.
- "Publish again" makes a new public row with a new token; the revoked row stays, so the
  old URL keeps showing the inactive page rather than "does not match any project". The
  card, the project list, the sends and the personal-row inserts read the newest public
  row of the newest instrument; the open personal links take the new dates at once; a
  date save on a revoked link is refused until Publish again. A stale tab's Revoke names
  the row it showed and is refused when the link changed since.
- Revoking the public link does not touch the personal links (E6-2's open point): they
  were sent to named people and are revoked one by one. Sending new invites and reminders
  is refused while the public link is revoked, since the personal links take its dates.
- Revoke on a personal row sets its revoked_at; the row reads Revoked with the date; the
  token shows the inactive page; the person cannot be reminded; pasting the address again
  says to press New link on its row (a revoked Not sent row cannot be claimed either).
  "New link" gives the same row a fresh token (so the address, name, role, responses and
  reminder history stay) with the public link's dates, read under the same locks and
  checks as a send, and sends email 2 at once; the old token then reads as unknown, since the row no longer
  carries it. A failed email leaves the row Not sent, to be pasted again.
- The respondent app learns of a revoke through GET /r/[token]/state: a status and one
  word (200 open, notOpen or passcode; 404 unknown; 410 revoked or closed), never project
  data. The open page polls it every 60 seconds, and at once when a background tab comes
  to the front, and refreshes itself on anything but 200 open; the server then renders the
  inactive, closed or passcode page. E7-3's autosave route will run the same check before a
  write and answer 410 without writing (stories/E7-3, acceptance 7).
- The Share page never carries a personal token: the stale-tab check on a row's Revoke
  uses a 16-character hash of it (linkMark). A stale tab's date Save names the public row
  it showed, as Revoke does, and is refused once Publish again replaced that row.

## What was not decided

- Deleting the answers given through a revoked link: out of scope in R1.
- A confirmation step on Revoke link: docs/review-list.md.
