# Design note 35: the mode sweep, 2026-10-03

Mihai, after the design v2 merge: "improve the animation that happens when switching from
dark mode to light mode. It should be a gradual change top left corner to bottom right
corner, like pulling a blanket folding it over, and it should be like 1 to 2 seconds long."

## What was built

- The toggle (src/components/app/mode-toggle.tsx) wraps the class flip in
  document.startViewTransition, the View Transition API
  (developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Document/startViewTransition; Baseline since
  October 2025 per that page). The browser snapshots the page before and after the flip
  and animates between the two snapshots.
- The animation (src/app/globals.css, mode-sweep): the old snapshot keeps still (its
  default fade is removed) and the new snapshot wears a mask three times the page in size,
  a 135 degree gradient opaque for 44 percent, soft over 12 percent, then transparent. The
  mask moves from its transparent end to its opaque end over 2,500 ms (1,400 at first; Mihai
asked for 2.5 seconds after seeing it) with an ease in and
  out, so a soft diagonal edge travels from the top left corner to the bottom right and the
  new mode appears behind it, like a blanket pulled across. The edge is soft rather than a
  hard fold, which reads better over text.
- Scoped to an html class the toggle sets for the duration, so no other view transition
  inherits the mask.
- Off under prefers-reduced-motion (the toggle then flips at once) and in a browser without
  startViewTransition (same). No other behaviour changes; the stored choice and the script
  before paint are as in design note 34.

## Checks

Typecheck, lint, the styleguide Playwright test (the toggle still flips, persists and
follows the system setting; in Chromium the flip happens inside the transition's update
callback, so the assertions see the class at once). The motion itself is checked by eye in
the browser; a test of the mask's position would test the browser, not the product.

## Mihai's second look, 2026-10-03

"There is no animation on the toggle, it has two states and changes two seconds later; the
ball is not centred." Cause: the toggle sat inside the page snapshot, so its thumb only
changed when the sweep reached the bottom left corner of the sidebar, about two seconds in.
Fix: the track and the thumb carry their own view-transition-name (MDN: an element with a
name is captured as its own group and animated apart from the root), with their groups at
150 ms, so the thumb slides at once while the page sweeps behind it. The thumb is centred
with a vertical translate instead of a fixed top offset, which the 1 px border had pushed
1 px low.

## Mihai's third look, 2026-10-03

"There is also a delay: if you press the toggle, it waits some time, then the animation
starts." Measured on the production build in Chromium (a script timing
document.startViewTransition on /styleguide, four toggles): the transition is ready 59 to
71 ms after the click, and the thumb and the sweep start together at that moment. The wait
was in the geometry, not in the browser. Frames captured every 250 ms and compared with the
old mode: 0.1 percent of pixels changed (the thumb) until 983 ms, 54 percent at 1,240 ms,
95.7 percent from 1,523 ms. With a mask three times the page, the edge (the 44 to 56 percent
band of the gradient) is inside the page only while the mask position is between 25 and 75
percent of its travel, and the ease in and out spent the first 0.9 s and the last 0.9 s
outside that range. Fix: mask-size 230 percent (the edge is off the page by under 1
percent of the travel at each end) and a linear curve. Frames after the fix: 1.4 percent
at 255 ms, 7.8 at 470, 30.8 at 833, 47.9 at 1,061, 63.3 at 1,287, 87.9 at 1,648, 94.7 at
1,849, 95.7 from 2,084 ms, the soft tail to 2,500 ms. The other curves tried (ease in and
out, cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1), ease, a sine curve) all crossed in under 1.3 s or
started late. The thumb's 150 ms groups are unchanged.

## Mihai's fourth look, 2026-10-03

"If the change from dark mode to light mode starts from top left, the change back from
light to dark should be in reverse from bottom right." The toggle adds mode-sweep-to-dark
beside mode-sweep when the next mode is dark; that rule turns the gradient to 315 degrees
and runs the travel the other way (mode-sweep-back), so the edge starts at the bottom right
corner and ends at the top left. Same size, curve and 2.5 s. Checked with the frame script
split into quadrants: going dark, the bottom right quadrant changes first (4 percent at
256 ms, 88 at 858, 99 at 1,174) and the top left last (0 until 1,295 ms); going light, the
reverse (top left 7 percent at 249 ms, 93 at 1,012; bottom right 0 until 1,268 ms). The
first run right after the production server started showed the page changed at once in the
to-dark direction; five later runs, two with the identical script, showed the sweep. The
cause was not found. If Mihai sees a flip without a sweep on his PC, this is the place to
look.
