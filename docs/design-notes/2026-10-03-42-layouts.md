Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-03 for stories/E5-3, under decision 0044.

## What was decided

- The three layouts sit on the Scoring card under the labels, as three radio cards like the
  methods (the board has them in the same place), saved with the same Save.
- Once an instrument is published the layout still changes: it shapes the screens and
  changes nothing stored. The method, the switch and the labels stay locked (E5-2).
  Recorded in docs/review-list.md.
- The preview's Items screen follows the layout: the chapter row (About you and every area,
  the first active) with the first area's cards; one card with "Item 1 of [N] in [AREA]";
  or every area in order with "All [N] on one page" and no chapter row. Ten cards in all
  across the areas, the line under them saying so. The chapter row and the rating rows
  carry the ring.
- The phone check of acceptance 3 runs in the preview frame until the respondent app
  exists (E7-2): no side scroll in the 390 px frame (its scrollWidth), every pill at least
  38 px, on a fresh project rather than the sample. The pills keep the 38 px of the design
  system (decision 0018 item 4 said 36 and left the size to Mihai) rather than the 48 px
  the story line asked for: a 48 px row plus its captions leaves the 260 px card no room
  for the details slot. Recorded in docs/review-list.md; E7-2 repeats the check at 375 by
  667 on the real screens (its acceptance 4).
- The chapter row in the preview shows About you, every area and Wrap up as a picture:
  free navigation between chapters and the two card columns on desktop are E7-4's and
  E7-2's (the preview is phone-only); the ring on the chapter row is always on in the
  chapters and item layouts, since Build is the step that changes it, and the page layout
  has no row to ring. The layout cards follow the v1 PM app board (PmApp.dc.html); the v2
  board has no layout control yet.
- Published instruments: the server reads only the layout then; a locked fieldset posts
  nothing for the method (the form data set skips disabled controls), and whatever a stale
  tab posts for the method, the switch or the labels is ignored rather than refused.
- Audit of 2026-10-03 (21 findings, 3 blocking): the published form could not save its
  layout (the disabled method radios posted nothing), the labels comparison depended on
  jsonb key order (removed with the rule above), the reassigned budget counter in the
  server component (CI's lint), the Wrap up pill, items without an area in the page
  layout, the per-area "first N of M" line, the layout in the card key, the caption, the
  chapter row's fade, the fieldset descriptions, the test asserting at least one pill.
