# Design note 01: direction, 2026-09-30

Superseded in part by note 02 (typeface, backgrounds, accent, imagery), written after viewing
duna.com in a browser. Where the two disagree, note 02 wins.

References named by Mihai: duna.com (liked a lot), trello.com (liked). No reference to avoid
was named; the default to avoid is the median AI-generated SaaS look (Inter everywhere,
indigo accent, gradient blobs, three-column feature grids with icons).

What was read on 2026-09-30, from the public pages as text (visual sampling with a browser
is still to do in Claude Code):

Duna. One-statement hero headline, one sentence under it, one button. Customer logos as a
quiet strip. A stats block with three large multipliers and short labels. Product sections that
alternate: a short heading, one line, one link, then four feature tiles of a title and one
sentence. Customer quotes with name and title. A trust section. News cards with reading time.
Built in Framer, product screens used as the imagery. The tone is calm and declarative; every
section says one thing.

Trello. Mega-menu navigation, hero with sign-up form and product video, product screens in a
slider, feature sections with spot illustrations, testimonial carousel with statistics, plan
strip, footer with locale switcher. Friendlier, illustrated, more colour, more motion.

Decision: Duna's structure and restraint for both the marketing site and the PM app; Trello's
warmth only in the respondent journey, where finishing matters more than looking serious.
No illustration set in R1; real product screens are the art.

## Type

Headlines: Instrument Serif (Google Fonts). A serif at large sizes gives the editorial,
considered tone Duna has without copying its typeface, and it is what separates the site from
the sans-everywhere default. Used for page titles and marketing headlines only.

UI and body: Geist Sans (Vercel, open licence). Neutral, tight, good at 13 px in dense tables
and at 17 px on a phone.

Numbers: Geist Mono with tabular figures, in the dashboard and stats blocks only.

Scale (px): 12, 13, 14, 16, 17, 20, 24, 32, 44, 64. PM app base 14; respondent base 17;
marketing body 17, headlines 44 and 64.

## Colour

Neutrals: warm paper background (#F6F5F2), surface white (#FFFFFF), ink (#16181C), muted ink
(#5B6069), hairline (#E6E4DF). Dark mode for the PM app: background #111214, surface #1A1C1F,
ink #F2F1EE.

Brand accent, one only: deep teal (#0E6B63). Used for primary actions, links, focus rings and
the wordmark. Never used for data.

Data colours, semantic, fixed across the product and never reused for brand:
agree #2F855A, pushed back #B7791F, unclear #6B46C1, missing #2B6CB0, not needed #718096.
Each has a light tint for backgrounds and a 4.5:1 text variant.

## Space and shape

4 px base, steps 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96. Content max width 1120 px on marketing,
1440 px in the app with a 240 px sidebar. Radius: 6 px in the PM app, 12 px on respondent cards
and buttons, full round for pills. Borders 1 px hairline; shadows only on floating elements
(menus, dialogs), one shadow token.

## Density

PM side: dense. 14 px text, 36 px row heights, tables by default, sidebar navigation, keyboard
shortcuts from R2. Respondent side: roomy. 17 px text, 48 px tap targets, one thing per screen
on a phone, progress always visible, no sidebar.

## Motion

150 ms ease-out for state changes, 250 ms for page and item transitions. Progress bars fill.
Live dashboard updates fade the changed cell, no counters spinning. No bounces, no parallax,
no hero animations. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns all of it off.

## Voice

Plain, second person, present tense, no exclamation marks. Buttons are verbs. The marketing
site states what the product does and shows it; it does not promise outcomes it has not
measured. See WRITING.md.

## Respondent side specifics

Follows the PM's brand: logo and accent from workspace settings, on the neutral base above.
No dark mode in R1. Serif is not used on the respondent side; every screen is Geist at 17 px.

## To verify with a browser before step 2

Sample Duna's actual type and palette to calibrate weight and contrast, not to copy. Check
Instrument Serif at 64 px on a 375 px phone (may need 40 px). Confirm the teal passes 4.5:1
on the paper background for 14 px text.

## Rejected

Inter as the UI face (default of every generated site). Indigo or violet accent (same reason).
Gradient blobs and glassmorphism. An illustration system in R1 (cost, and product screens do
the job). Dark mode on the respondent side (respondents get the PM's brand, not ours).
