# Design note 74: the legal pages, 2026-10-04

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-04 for stories/E11-3, under decision 0044.

## What was decided

- The four pages are Markdown files in docs/legal/, so a change is a commit and the lawyer reads
  plain files. Each starts with its version and date. A small parser (src/lib/legal.ts) reads
  headings, lists and paragraphs and turns the markers, the /legal/ addresses and the contact
  address into parts; nothing is rendered as HTML.
- The pages use the app's type on the ground colour in one 760 px column, with the lockup at the
  top, "Version [N], [DATE]" under it, and the four pages linked at the bottom.
- Markers show in the sun tint, so a reader of a draft sees what is not confirmed.
- What the drafts state about the product is taken from the code (the cookies and their
  lengths, the session's 30 days, what Shape and Write actions send, the 24 hour deletion, the
  exports) and from docs/accounts.md (the regions). Where the code or the accounts cannot say
  (legal names, Anthropic's region and terms, backup retention, the authority's details), the
  text carries a marker.
- The respondent's privacy link sits beside Powered by on About you and Done, and shows on a
  paid plan too, where Powered by does not.

## Components added

- The legal page layout (src/app/legal/[page]/page.tsx): no new design-system component.
