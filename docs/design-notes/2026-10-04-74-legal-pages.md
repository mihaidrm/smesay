# Design note 74: the legal pages, 2026-10-04

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-04 for stories/E11-3, under decision 0044.

## What was decided

- The four pages are Markdown files in docs/legal/, so a change is a commit and the lawyer reads
  plain files. Each starts with its version and date. A small parser (src/lib/legal.ts) reads
  headings, lists and paragraphs and turns the markers and the /legal/ addresses into parts;
  nothing is rendered as HTML. hello@smesay.app stays text until the domain is bought
  (docs/copy/landing.md).
- The pages use the app's type on the ground colour in one 760 px column, with the lockup at the
  top, "Version [N], [DATE]" under it, and the four pages linked at the bottom.
- Markers show in the sun tint, so a reader of a draft sees what is not confirmed.
- What the drafts state about the product is taken from the code (the cookies and their
  lengths, the session's 30 days, what Shape and Write actions send, the exports) and from
  docs/accounts.md (the regions). The launch providers are described as planned: none of those
  accounts exists yet (decision 0006). The 24 hour removal holds only once the removal job runs
  every hour on the host, so every line that promises it carries a marker. Where the code or the accounts cannot say
  (legal names, Anthropic's region and terms, backup retention, the authority's details), the
  text carries a marker.
- The respondent's privacy link sits beside Powered by on About you and Done, and shows on a
  paid plan too, where Powered by does not.

## Components added

- The legal page layout (src/app/legal/[page]/page.tsx): no new design-system component.

## Audit

The fresh-context audit found 8 blocking statements the code contradicted, 10 should-fix and 7
nits. The drafts now list what is stored and sent as the code does it: a personal invite's
fields, the perspectives, the closing answer and the device record; the session's IP address
and user agent, the Google picture and tokens, the sign-in and request counts held in memory until a restart or a full store,
the browser storage on both sides; the passcode's 15 minutes; what Write actions send, free
text as typed included; members.csv. The terms name the monthly AI allowance (decision 0036).
The landing question form is gone from the policy (E12-5 is deferred). The DPA names the two
reads that span workspaces, and the lines on logs, confidentiality and the 24 hours carry
markers. The parser keeps text after a heading and wrapped list items, reads one level of
brackets inside a marker, refuses an impossible date and links a /legal/ address only when it
stands on its own. Placeholders such as the registered address are markers now.

The re-check found 4 more blocking points, fixed: the session record and the in-memory counts
are not kept for a set time (the policy now says when they go, with markers); the DPA no longer
claims backups before E11-4 builds them; and acceptance 1 no longer asks for the landing
questions, which E12-5 adds when it is built. Also: the product-wide AI limit in the terms, the
respondents' workspace in the DPA, encryption in transit as planned, account retention, what
Shape does with its grouping, and the sign-in link record.
