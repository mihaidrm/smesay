# Design note 48: reminders, 2026-10-03

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-03 for stories/E6-3, under decision 0044.
Files: src/lib/reminders.ts and reminders-rules.ts (the rule and the words),
src/lib/mail/reminder-email.ts (email 3), src/db/queries/invites.ts (claimReminder,
unclaimReminder), responses.forInvite, answers.countForResponse, the Share page
(share/remind-buttons.tsx, invites-card.tsx), src/lib/sharing-format.ts (formatUtc moved
out of the database module so client parts can use it).

## What was decided

- Two buttons and nothing else sends a reminder: Remind on a row and "Remind everyone
  who has not submitted" over the list. No job, no schedule (docs/copy/emails.md: never
  automatic). The line over the list says so.
- The rule, checked from the row for the screen and again in one update statement for
  the send: the invite was sent, is not revoked, the person has not submitted, and the
  last reminder is 72 hours old or more (none yet counts). Under 72 hours the row shows
  "Reminded [DAYS] days ago. The next reminder can go on [DATE]." in place of the
  button; a submitted person and a Not sent row show nothing there. The story's "three
  days" is 72 hours, so 71 hours refuses and 72 allows.
- The claim adds one to reminders_sent and sets last_reminder_at before the email goes,
  and refuses when the invite's newest response (the one the list reads) is submitted;
  the newest response is read again after the claim, since the statement sees only what
  was committed before it started. A failed email, or anything that throws after the
  claim, puts both back. Two presses at once send one email; the loser reads the row
  again and gets the too-soon line with the address in front, or "[EMAIL] was not
  reminded: another request changed the row just now..." when the row reads due again
  (the other request's email failed and gave the claim back). A stale tab's Remind on a submitted person, a Not
  sent row or a revoked invite reads "[EMAIL] cannot be reminded: ..."
- "Remind everyone" is one request over the rows due now, one outcome per person; the
  people not due are left out, "Nobody is due a reminder." disables the button, and a
  stale tab's press with nobody due gets the same line as its outcome.
- Email 3's branch comes from the invite's newest response and its answer rows: no
  response or no answer reads "You have not started yet.", else "You answered [N] of
  [M] items." with M the set's item count. The subject names the close day in UTC; a
  link with no close date drops the clause.
- The link in force must be published and open or opening later, as for sending an
  invite; the same three refusals apply.

## What was not decided

- Reminding through the public link (no address): not in R1.
- A reminder the day before the close date, sent by the app: out of scope (scheduled
  reminders).
