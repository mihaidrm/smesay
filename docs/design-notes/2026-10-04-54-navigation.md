# Design note 54: chapter row, progress and the Wrap up's gaps, 2026-10-04

Made in the Claude Code cloud session of 2026-10-04 for stories/E7-4, under decision 0044.

## What was decided

- The chapter row sits under the respondent header on every screen once the respondent has
  started, About you included (so they go back without pressing Start), the bar under it.
  Pills are 32 px high with a 48 px hit area (the row's padding keeps it
  inside the scroll box), scroll sideways when the list is long, and bring the active one
  into view. The active pill takes the workspace's accent with white text; the
  others are white with a hairline. On the single long page only the bar shows. Departures
  from the respondent board, recorded in docs/review-list.md: the board drew the active
  pill in the accent's soft tint with accent text at 12 px and 30 px high, and a mono
  "14 of 40" with the bar; the code fills the pill with the accent (the strongest "you are
  here") and leaves the count to the pills and the progressbar's numbers. A pill's count
  reads "[done] of [count] answered" to a screen reader; the "0/2" drawn is hidden from it.
- The row, the bar, the footer count and the Wrap up count what the server holds
  complete. The answers route already returns `complete`; the saver keeps it per item from
  the reply with the highest version (doneFrom, src/lib/answer-queue.ts), so the row never
  shows a count this page has not had confirmed. Answers saved on another device or in another window show on the next load. A
  card that is complete on the device but not yet saved shows "Not saved yet" in the Wrap up
  list.
- Continue is never disabled. The last chapter, and the single long page, continue to the
  Wrap up.
- "Welcome back" appears over the screen a returning visit lands on (the story's
  acceptance 4), not on About you as the board drew it: the visit lands on the chapter, or
  on the Wrap up when every item is complete, and the box goes once the respondent moves.
  It shows whenever the response holds an answer, complete or not (landingOf).
- The Wrap up on the live link uses the Build preview's component with the gaps, a Go to
  button for the first gap (it wraps on a phone when the area's name is long), the "Still
  to finish" list and Back. Each row opens its own item: the item itself in the one-item
  layout, otherwise its card scrolled into view with focus on its first control.
- A screen change moves focus to the new screen's heading (tabIndex -1), so a screen
  reader hears where it landed; the single long page has a heading for this that only a
  screen reader sees. Before, focus fell to the page when the button pressed went away. The tally, the form, confidence, the sign-off and Submit are not shown on the live
  link until E7-5 makes them work (the audit found a Submit with no action and a form that
  kept nothing).
