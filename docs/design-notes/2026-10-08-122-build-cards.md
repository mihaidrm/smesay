# 122 Build's cards, one open at a time, 2026-10-08

Mihai, 2026-10-08, after the Import and Shape pages got their collapsible cards (design note
110): "the build phase is also so huge on the screen, i am sure you could compact it into
something much friendlier to use". Build showed five cards open at once (Intro, Scoring,
Perspectives, Closing, Respondent fields), about 1,900 px of forms beside the preview, with
the Scoring card alone holding three rows of radio cards, a switch and five label fields.

Decided:
- The five cards are the collapsible card of design note 110 (src/components/app/
  collapsible-card.tsx), so Build reads like Import: a title row per card, a click opens it,
  the content stays in the DOM while closed and the open and close animate. The "Build on
  version N" card above them stays a plain card.
- One open at a time: the five share the details element's name attribute, which makes them
  an exclusive group (developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/HTML/Reference/Elements/details, the
  name attribute; browser-compat-data read on 2026-10-08: Chrome 120, Firefox 130, Safari
  17.2). Opening a card closes the open one, so the page never grows back to five open forms;
  an older browser leaves each card to open on its own. Mihai asked for this on Import too
  ("one open at a time"); note 110 kept both Preview and Column mapping open there because a
  PM compares them. On Build no two cards are read together: each is its own form with its
  own Save, and the preview on the right shows the result.
- Which card opens, src/lib/build-guide.ts (openBuildCards, with its unit test): Intro while
  nothing is written in it, since the intro is the one thing every validation needs; once an
  intro is saved, no card, and the page is the list of settings; nothing on the sample, which
  cannot be edited. The step tip "Write one or two lines..." (E15-3) still shows above while
  the intro is empty.
- A card holds its state through a save (the card's new `hold`: the open prop is read on
  mount only). The saves on Build revalidate the page, so without it the Intro card would
  snap closed the moment its intro was saved, with "Saved." inside it. Import keeps the
  other behaviour, where the data moves the cards (the check closes after the import and
  Versions opens).
- Each title row carries the card's settings, from the instrument, so the closed page says
  what respondents get (BUILD_CARD_COPY in src/lib/build-guide.ts; docs/copy/app.md, Build):
  Intro: "[TITLE]. [INTRO]" or "[TITLE]. No intro yet."; Scoring: "MoSCoW, proposal shown, a
  reason when the answer differs, chapters" (the method, the switch, the reason rule and the
  layout in that order); Perspectives: "Finance, Sales. 2 of 6 items carry a perspective." or
  "None. Every item goes to everyone."; Closing: "No closing question. Asks for missing items.
  Confidence always on."; Respondent fields: "2 fields" in mono beside the title and "Name
  and Role. Both required." ("Name, Role and Team. 2 of 3 required.", "All required.", "All
  optional."). The lines that sat under each title ("Pick how respondents rate each item...")
  are the first paragraph inside the card.
- The unsaved changes guard (E5-9) works as before: the "Not saved" label is the card's
  `mark`, on the title row, so it shows on a closed card too; the danger border and the shake
  are on the card element; a click on the title row opens the card to Save or Discard. The
  Closing card keeps data-preview-screen="wrap", so a click on its title row, which focuses
  the row, opens the Wrap up in the preview as its fields did.

Rejected: a wizard through the five cards with Next buttons (the PM loses the overview, and
four of the five cards are fine at their defaults); tabs inside one card (no settings in view
while another tab is open); denser forms in place (segmented controls instead of the radio
cards with their hints: at most a third off a page that stays five forms tall); opening the
Respondent fields card while Name and Role are the defaults (the tip of E15-3 already says
so, and a PM happy with the defaults would find it open on every visit).

Tests: src/lib/build-guide.test.ts (the open card, the list joiner, the five summaries, 9
tests); e2e/build.spec.ts asserts Intro open and the four others closed with their
summaries on the first open, the Intro card still open with the saved intro on its row after
Save, Intro closed once the fields card opens, every card closed after a reload with an
intro, and the summaries after the scoring, perspectives, fields and closing saves;
e2e/unsaved-guard.spec.ts finds the Intro card by its test id; e2e/respondent-rate.spec.ts
opens the Scoring card before changing the layout.
