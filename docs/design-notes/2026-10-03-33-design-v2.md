# Design note 33: design v2, alive in two modes, 2026-10-03

Decision 0041. Mihai's brief: the product feels bland and soulless; he wants life in it,
Dripify's feel as the reference (dark surfaces, one bright accent, rounded cards, badges with
counts, a sidebar with icons), both modes, my palette, bought assets where I cannot draw.
Boards on the canvas: Brand07 (the system), LandingF, PmAppV2, RespondentV2, in the row
"Design v2" under the superseded landing pages. Proposal, nothing coded yet.

## What gives it life, in order of weight

1. Depth. Cards float on a tinted ground with one soft violet shadow instead of sitting flat
   behind a hairline. Dark mode gets a raised surface for the layer above the card.
2. One warm colour against one cool one. Violet carries the brand; coral marks counts, the
   disagreement view and what needs attention; mint says saved and agreed; sun says pushed
   back. v1 had teal alone and ink buttons, which is why it read as grey.
3. A gradient in two places only: the hero's aurora (violet, coral, sun at low opacity on
   navy) and the primary button. Never on text in the app; once per marketing page as a
   text gradient on the headline's second half.
4. Rounder, bolder type. Plus Jakarta Sans at 700 and 800 for headings with tight tracking
   replaces Geist 500. Numbers stay in Geist Mono.
5. Living details: a pulsing dot on "arriving now", stat tiles with the number at 30 px,
   avatars as coloured initials, an area header with its one-line rationale, the sample card
   in the sidebar.
6. A mascot in four places (hero, sign-in, empty states, the respondent's thank-you) once it
   is bought; a violet blob with the two speech marks until then.

## Motion

Modest, as asked. Marketing: sections rise 18 px and fade in over 700 ms, once, cards
staggered; the "arriving now" dot pulses every 2.2 s; the mascot floats 8 px over 6 s; cards
lift 3 px on hover with a deeper shadow. App: cards lift 2 px on hover; the live dot pulses;
the stepper's active pill slides between steps over 250 ms; progress bars fill over 250 ms;
the mode toggle's thumb slides over 150 ms; a changed dashboard cell fades over 400 ms (v1
rule kept). Nothing loops except the live dot and the mascot, and both stop under
prefers-reduced-motion, where every reveal is visible at once. No counters that spin.

## Mihai's review of the first boards (2026-10-03)

Direction good. Three asks, all done the same day: the v2 row sat under the superseded
landing pages A to D and the phone board, which reach down to y 12,180 on the canvas, so the
row moved to y 12,600; the boards were rendered in Chromium at their sizes and the overflows
fixed (the hero now centres in its height with the card at 540 px, mono references do not
wrap, the respondent's five pills fit at 10 px, the phone footer stays in view, long chapter
rows fade out at the edge, every card sizes to its content instead of stretching); modest
animation added (above); dark lightened one step (tokens above, contrast recomputed). The
whole landing page stays dark hero and pricing with light sections; "a little lighter
overall" is read as the tokens, not the layout. Mihai also asked for the full list of links
before buying anything: docs/assets.md.
